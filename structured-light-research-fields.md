# Potential research fields — Structured Light
<!-- last updated: 2026-07-05 — synthesized from the chapters Frank flagged in the Roadmap on Structured Light (Rubinsztein-Dunlop, Forbes et al., J. Opt. 19 (2017) 013001) -->

This maps the 12 *Roadmap* chapters Frank flagged onto candidate PhD research directions, grouped into four themes, each tied back to his existing signature (SLM mode shaping, singular optics, Poincaré / higher-order Poincaré spheres, optical analogs) and to the US PIs now on the target list.

## Frank's anchor
His recurring intellectual reflex — **"lift into a higher-dimensional space to reveal hidden structure"** (Koopman → Fourier-plane encoding → Poincaré-sphere extension) — is *the* native language of structured light: every degree of freedom (phase, polarization, OAM, time) is a coordinate on some sphere or manifold. Structured light is where his taste and his hands already meet.

---

## Theme A — Foundations: singularities, angular momentum, and geometry
**Roadmap chapters:** 2 (Vortices, natural & deliberate — Berry & Dennis) · 3 (Structured light photonics & light–matter — Andrews) · 5 (Electromagnetic angular momentum — Mansuripur).

- **What it is:** Optical vortices as phase singularities (nodal lines where phase is undefined), the spin/orbital decomposition of the EM field's angular momentum, and the still-unsettled fundamentals of how structured light exchanges momentum with matter.
- **Open problems:** the canonical vs. kinetic momentum debate; near-field/transverse spin; angular momentum in non-paraxial and structured fields.
- **PhD directions:** measuring/engineering singular skeletons of 3D fields; transverse spin dynamics in tightly focused beams; optical-analog experiments that make abstract momentum/geometry visible on a table (a direct continuation of Frank's gravitational-lensing / quantum-pendulum analogs).
- **PIs:** **Alonso (Rochester)** — the geometry/mathematics of structured beams (ray-wave-polarization, Majorana–Poincaré sphere); **Litchinitser (Duke)** — singular↔nonlinear optics, optical knots; **Swartzlander (RIT)** — optical vortices.

## Theme B — Polarization structure & vector fields
**Roadmap chapters:** 7 (Structured polarization at the nanoscale — Banzer & Bauer) · 8 (Space-varying polarized fields: classical & quantum — Karimi & Marrucci).

- **What it is:** Fields whose polarization varies across the beam (vector/Poincaré beams, full-Poincaré beams, 3D polarization topologies — Möbius strips, skyrmions), their measurement at the nanoscale, and their classical *and* quantum (spin-orbit-coupled photon) uses.
- **This is Frank's closest existing turf** — higher-order Poincaré spheres and vector-beam structure are exactly the "provisional missing-variable" hypothesis from his Open Questions cluster.
- **PhD directions:** generation/measurement of full-Poincaré and 3D-polarization-topological fields; spin–orbit photonics as a qudit resource; polarization singularities in tightly focused / nanostructured settings.
- **PIs:** **Alonso (Rochester)** (full-Poincaré beams, transverse spin); **Otte (Rochester)** (structured singular light + nanoscale light–matter); **Capasso (Harvard)** (metasurface structured polarization, single-shot Mueller imaging). *(Karimi/Marrucci are the field leaders but are non-US → excluded per Frank's scope.)*

## Theme C — Making & applying structured light: sources, cold atoms, communication
**Roadmap chapters:** 6 (Shaping light — Denz & Alpmann) · 22 (Tailoring light at the source — Forbes) · 12 (Structured light + ultracold atoms — Bigelow) · 18 (Quantum communication — Fickler) · 19 (Classical optical communication — Willner & Xie).

- **What it is:** The toolchain (SLMs, DMDs, q-plates, metasurfaces, structured lasers) and the two biggest application arenas — (i) using structured light to control the *motion and quantum state of ultracold atoms* and build singular-optic analogues, and (ii) using OAM/spatial modes as high-dimensional channels for classical and quantum communication.
- **This theme carries Frank's networking-overlap career track** and his SLM tooling directly.
- **PhD directions:** imprinting full-Bloch/Poincaré textures onto BECs (structured-light–matter topology); structured light generated *at the source* (on-chip vortex lasers); high-dimensional OAM QKD and mode-multiplexed comms; structured light through turbulence/fiber.
- **PIs:** **Bigelow (Rochester)** — structured light + cold atoms & optical analogs (the single best fit); **Willner (USC)** — OAM classical comms; **Gauthier (OSU)** & **Ramachandran (BU)** — structured-light quantum comms (free-space and fiber); **Feng (Penn)** — on-chip structured-light sources.

## Theme D — Quantum information & structured light in time
**Roadmap chapters:** 17 (QIP with structured light — Romero & White) · 21 (Structuring light in time — Weiner).

- **What it is:** Using the high-dimensional Hilbert space of spatial/OAM modes for quantum information processing (qudits, high-dimensional entanglement), and extending "structuring" from space into **time/frequency** — pulse shaping, spatiotemporal optical vortices (STOVs), transverse OAM, attosecond structured light.
- **Open frontier:** space-time coupled fields; light carrying momentum in genuinely new (transverse-OAM) ways; up-conversion of structured light into the EUV/attosecond regime.
- **PhD directions:** high-dimensional entangled-photon experiments; STOV generation/measurement; structured light in the ultrafast/nonlinear regime.
- **PIs:** **Milchberg (UMD)** — STOVs / transverse OAM; **Murnane & Kapteyn (JILA)** — structured light in HHG/EUV, attosecond skyrmions; **Alù (CUNY)** — time-metamaterials; **Ramachandran/Gauthier** — high-dimensional structured-photon QI. *(Romero/White and Weiner: Australia / deceased → not PhD targets.)*
